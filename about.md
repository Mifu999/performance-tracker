# Performance Tracker

Records **FPS, CPU and RAM** while you play and keeps the history, so you can
look back at *when* the game ran badly and *which level* was responsible.

Open the panel from the button in the bottom row of the main menu.

- **Overview** - averages, 1% lows, best and worst moments, time under 30/60 FPS
- **Graph** - FPS / CPU / RAM over time, with hour labels
- **Levels** - your levels sorted heaviest first
- **Sessions** - one row per launch, with crash detection

Everything is stored locally and can be exported to CSV.

CPU and RAM readings are available on Windows and Android; on macOS and iOS only
FPS is recorded.
